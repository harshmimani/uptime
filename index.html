Five hostel rules

  1. Protein first. Two katoris of dal, a bowl of curd, and paneer, chana, rajma or soya whenever it’s served.
  2. One carb, not two. 2 rotis or 1 katori of rice per meal. An extra serving only after track and long-run days.
  3. Skip the oil on top. Dry sabzi over gravy. Fried snacks once a week at most.
  4. One handful of nuts and seeds a day, in total. Healthy but dense: 30 g of almonds is about 170 kcal.
  5. Drink water, not calories. Sugared chai once or twice a day at most, no juice.

The plate

  • Half: sabzi and salad. Fill up here first.
  • A quarter: protein. Dal, rajma, chana, paneer, soya or curd.
  • A quarter: grains. 2 rotis or 1 katori rice, not both.

Sleep is part of the diet

In a two-week study, people on the same diet who got 5.5 hours of sleep instead of 8.5 lost about half as much fat and more muscle. Your 7 to 7.5 hours is good; aim for 8 on training nights.

Training in humidity

  • Drink 500 ml before training and 3–3.5 litres across the day.
  • On run days and sweaty sessions, have salted buttermilk or nimbu paani with a pinch of salt and sugar.
  • Runs feel 20–30 seconds per km slower in humid air. That’s the weather, not lost fitness.

Shopping list

  • Whey: one scoop a day, two on short days. A 1 kg tub lasts three to four weeks.
  • Roasted chana and pumpkin seeds
  • Anjeer, munakka and chia for pre-training
  • Bananas, a few at a time
  • Almonds: one small handful a day, so a 250 g pack lasts over a week

How it works

Four days a week: two full-body strength sessions, a track session and a long run, with a rest day in the middle of the week. Built to stick to, not to impress.

Belly fat, honestly

You can’t burn fat from one spot. A 2022 meta-analysis of 13 studies found that training a muscle doesn’t shrink the fat over it. Where fat comes off first is set by genes, sex and age, and for many men the lower belly and the sides go last.

What works is overall fat loss: a small calorie deficit, high protein, strength training to keep your muscle, regular running and plenty of walking. The core work here (Pallof press, hanging knee raises, side planks, carries) builds and shapes the muscle underneath, so it shows when the fat comes off.

What to expect

  1. Weeks 1–2The scale swings by a kilo either way with water and food. Ignore it and learn the movements.
  2. Weeks 3–8About 0.3–0.5 kg a week off and 2–3 cm off the waist. Runs feel easier. First strict pull-up likely between weeks 6 and 12.
  3. Weeks 9–18A clear difference in photos and a flatter belly. The sides change last, which is normal. Sub-25 5K attempt at the end of block 3.
  4. From AprilEat at maintenance for your new weight and keep training. Ten pull-ups in a row is realistic by mid to late 2027.

    Why this order

    1. Power first. Jumps only build speed when you’re fresh.
    2. Pull-up practice next. It’s a skill, so it gets you before you’re tired.
    3. Big lifts, paired. A leg move and an upper-body move back to back saves time without stealing strength.
    4. Core near the end. A tired core can’t brace you under a heavy bar.
    5. Finisher last, and optional. It burns calories without costing the lifts. Skip it on a bad day.

    How heavy

    Pick a weight where the last rep of each set is hard but clean, with about two reps left in the tank. If your form breaks, it’s too heavy. Jumps are never about fatigue: every rep should be your fastest.

    Going heavier: once you hit the top of the rep range on every set, go up next session: 2.5 kg on a bar, the next dumbbell, or the next pin. Each lift’s card tells you when you’ve earned it. You won’t bulk up: you’re eating slightly under maintenance and lifting for strength, not size.

    Busy weeks and breaks

    Life will get in the way, especially when the new job starts. The minimum that holds what you’ve built: one strength session a week with one or two hard sets per lift at your normal weights, and one short run with some hard minutes. Strength has been shown to hold for months on that. Fat comes back through food, not missed workouts, so eat at maintenance and keep weighing in. Switch any week to break mode in Progress.

    Words you’ll see

    • Superset: two exercises back to back, then rest.
    • Tempo: comfortably hard running. You could say a few words, not a sentence.
    • Strides: 20 seconds building to fast but relaxed. Never a sprint to exhaustion.
    • EMOM: every minute on the minute. Work at the top of each minute, rest for the rest of it.
    • AMRAP: as many rounds as possible in the time. Tap +1 round as you go.
    • 21-15-9: 21 reps of each move, then 15, then 9, as fast as you can with good form.
    • Deload: the easy sixth week. Same weights, fewer sets.

    About the equipment

    • Squats run on the Smith machine with the safety stops set, since there’s no free squat rack.
    • Chest uses dumbbells on the adjustable bench instead of the crooked bench-press station.
    • Inverted rows use the Smith machine bar, locked at waist height.
    • Small floor: finishers stay on one spot. Shuttles and slides happen at the track.

    When to back off

    Sharp pain, pain that changes how you move, or pain that’s worse the next morning: stop that exercise, rest it, and see a doctor if it lingers. Ordinary muscle soreness for a day or two is normal, especially early on.

    The evidence

    Spot reduction doesn’t work

    Thirteen studies, 1,158 people: training one limb didn’t reduce the fat on it compared with the untrained side.

    Clemente & Afonso, Human Movement, 2022 ↗
    Lifting alone reduces body fat

    Across 58 studies using scans, strength training on its own cut body fat by about 1.4%, similar to cardio, while adding muscle.

    Wewege et al., Sports Medicine, 2022 ↗
    Intervals and steady running burn fat about equally

    Interval and moderate continuous training didn’t differ in their effect on weight, waist or fat mass. So the running here is chosen for your 5K and for basketball.

    Meta-analysis, 2023 ↗
    Lose slowly to keep muscle

    Losing about 0.5–1% of body weight a week keeps more muscle than faster loss. The plan aims for the gentle end.

    Helms et al., JISSN, 2014 ↗
    Protein protects muscle while dieting

    Eating more protein than the basic recommendation reduced lean mass loss during energy restriction.

    Hudson et al., Advances in Nutrition, 2020 ↗
    Processed food makes you eat more

    On an ultra-processed diet, people ate about 500 kcal a day more than on a minimally processed one, without trying to.

    Hall et al., Cell Metabolism, 2019 ↗
    Short sleep costs fat loss

    On the same diet, 5.5 hours of sleep instead of 8.5 cut the fat lost by 55% and increased muscle loss.

    Nedeltcheva et al., Annals of Internal Medicine, 2010 ↗
    The minimum dose to maintain

    Strength held for up to 32 weeks on one session and one set per exercise a week, as long as the weights stayed heavy. Endurance held on two short sessions a week at the same intensity.

    Spiering et al., JSCR, 2021 ↗
    Staying lean takes regular activity

    About 200–300 minutes a week of moderate activity is the guidance for keeping weight off, which is why daily walking counts.

    IARC Handbooks, physical activity and obesity ↗
    Habits take months, and simple wins

    A study of 30,000 gym-goers found the gym habit took about six months to form. In another, consistency, simple routines and enjoying the training predicted it.

    Buyalskaya et al., PNAS, 2023 ↗
